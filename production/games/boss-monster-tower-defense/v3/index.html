How to play

  • The march: Smaller monsters of the boss's own kind stream down the path toward your base 🏰. If one gets through, you lose a life — bigger monsters cost more lives, and the boss costs 10.
  • Between waves: Spend gold on towers. Beat all 5 waves — the boss walks the path on Wave 5, slow and tough, and it will use its signature move on your towers or itself.
  • Auto-send: Send Wave 1 yourself when your towers are ready. After that, once every monster on the board is eliminated, the next wave marches in on its own after a 3-second countdown — the line under the buttons counts it down, and you can spend that moment (and your wave-clear bonus gold) on towers. Turn it off any time with the Auto-send button.
  • Stacking waves: No waiting around — hit Send Wave again while a wave is still fighting and the next one marches in right behind it. Stack all 5 at once if you're feeling brave.
  • Upgrades: Tap any built tower to select it, then tap the round ⬆ button that pops up beside it to upgrade (up to Lv 3). You can also sell a selected tower back from the Selected panel.
  • The secret level: Beat the Sabertooth Tiger on Level 11 to unlock Level 12 — Glowing Eyes in the Darkness. It gets darker the longer that boss lives, and your towers' range shrinks in the dark.
