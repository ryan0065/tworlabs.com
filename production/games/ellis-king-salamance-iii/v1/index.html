Level 1 — 18 diamonds around the edge of the grass

  • Tap a blue diamond to flip it over and see what's hiding underneath.
  • Tap a second diamond. If the two match, both diamonds disappear!
  • If they don't match, they flip back — try to remember where everything is.
  • Two diamonds on every board hide tasty treats with no match. Flip one and King Salamance eats it on the spot — and if one diamond is ever left all alone, he eats that one too! 🦎
  • Level 2 doubles to 36 diamonds. After that, each new level adds just 10 more — 46, 56, 66… — and the extra diamonds fill in toward the middle of the grass, closer to the King.
  • The grass is mowed in a hatched checker pattern — use the squares to help remember where everything hides.
  • Miss more than five times in a row and the King gives you a royal reminder: he shows you one diamond you've already flipped before.
