How to Play
  • SOS Game — try to get a SOS.
  • First, pick profile pictures: Player One picks, then Player Two picks from the rest — the same picture can't be picked twice. Your picture pops up when your turn starts.
  • SOS = 1 pt. Every SOS you complete scores a point — across, down, or diagonal.
  • Take turns. Pick either S or O, then tap an empty square to place it.
  • Youngest goes first — in this game, that's Player One.
  • The game ends when the grid is full. Most points wins!
