RIDGE WORLD

Walk into Maple Lane — a made-up sample neighborhood built just for playing in.

No real streets, no real houses — just lawns, a park, and stacks of crates and blocks that are begging to be splatted, popped, and smashed. Everything you break flies apart with real bounce-and-tumble physics!

🎨 Paint Blaster — fast splats 🎈 Paint Balloon — big splash 🦠 Block Breaker — melts blocks apart 💥 Splash Bomb — the grenade-style paint popper: huge splash zone 🎯 Scope Blaster — long-range, super-accurate paint shots

All the blasters shoot paint and water — the "bombs" are splash bombs and the long-range one is a scoped paint blaster, so the whole neighborhood gets messy, never hurt.

HOW TO PLAY
  • Move: WASD, arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick (iPad & phone).
  • Aim & shoot: point with the mouse and click — or hold the big red FIRE button and Ridge aims at the nearest smashable thing.
  • Switch tools: keys 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5, or tap the tool buttons.
  • 2-player: Rhett tags along and shoots with you. Want to steer him yourself? Use the arrow keys while Ridge uses WASD.
  • Finish all 3 missions, then hit Challenge Mode in the pause menu: 90 seconds, most points wins bragging rights.
