DAD'S POOL GAME

Dad's in the big round pool. The boys are coming for him — unless it's Tag, and then nobody's safe!

🏊 18-FOOT ABOVE-GROUND POOL
1 — Who's jumping in?
2 — How do you want to play?
How to play — Throw Mode: Move Dad with WASD / Arrows or the joystick. When a boy grabs Dad, hit a throw button fast (or keys 1-5 / Space) before he dunks you! In trouble? Hit 💨 DASH (or Shift) to sprint away. Chain throws for big points. 🦈 If a shark shows up, there's only ONE way out: build a whirlpool — swim in circles, keep evading, and dash-push the shark to charge it up. The whirlpool washes the shark away and Dad scores +1000 survivor points!

⌨️ WASD / Arrows to swim • Shift or 💨 button to dash • 1-5 to throw • P to pause  •  📱 Drag joystick on phone / tablet
Psst… try swimming in circles around the pool. And flip the same boy 3 times in a row. Just sayin'. 👀 If a shark comes, only a whirlpool can save Dad!
